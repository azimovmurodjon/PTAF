⚠️ Risks & Dependencies Impacting Delivery

To ensure transparency, please note the following factors that may affect timelines:
	1.	Application Downtimes
	•	QA and automation activities are delayed when Navigator, Director, or Connects are unavailable.
	•	Example: A 2–3 day outage during a sprint can reduce automation throughput by ~30–40%.
	2.	Data Resets & Updates
	•	Test client data used for automation is often removed during environment refreshes.
	•	This requires recreation of data sets and re-validation of scripts, adding 2–5 extra days per sprint.
	3.	Access Issues
	•	Engineers sometimes lose access to Navigator, Director, or Connects.
	•	Even temporary loss blocks execution and script validation until resolved.
	4.	Vault Page Restrictions
	•	Contractors (non-FTEs) do not have access to Vault, which prevents them from testing flows that depend on Vault screens.
	•	This creates dependency on FTE engineers, lowering overall effective throughput.
